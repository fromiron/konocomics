## Method and evidence boundary

- Official publisher, serialization, creator, or exact-edition bibliographic evidence establishes identity and entry range first. Independent reviews are auxiliary and supply an Axis only when at least two source families repeat a concrete observation inside that range.
- Selection provenance, list membership, sales, ratings, awards, genre labels, and Theme labels are not Factor evidence. Their presence on a page was ignored.
- A source family is the publication or platform, not an individual account. Multiple accounts on BookLive, honto, Hatena Blog, or Rakuten Blog remain one family each. Copy-like observations were not counted twice.
- A known zero requires positive evidence of the repeated entry-range mechanism matching the zero anchor; silence is `unknown`. Midpoints are used only between the dictionary's 0/2/4 anchors.
- For position 21, claims from original standard volumes are used only after the publisher-supplied contents fields prove the frozen wide-volume bridge. For position 30, no bridge is needed or used: every volume-specific claim is tied directly to the frozen original Flower Comics volumes 1–3. Later editions and whole-series events are excluded.
- Candidate values below are proposals for a separate Pass B and Pass C. Unsupported values remain `unknown`; a candidate gate pass is not promotion.

Narrative order is `progression / problemSolving / strategy / pacing / mysteryReveal / worldBuilding`. Tone order is `characterArcWeight / relationshipStructure / comedy / darkness / mentalStress / romance / emotionalWarmth`. `U` means `unknown`, never a low value.
