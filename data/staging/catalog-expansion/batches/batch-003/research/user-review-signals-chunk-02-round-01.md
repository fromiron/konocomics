## Screening rule

A qualifying reviewer must have a visible native account or identifiable blog author, an exact early-entry boundary (normally volume 1 or an explicitly stated early-volume range), and a concrete repeated observation that maps to an unresolved text factor in the adjudicated queue. A volume-1 URL alone is not a stopping point. At least two genuinely independent reviewers must describe the same bounded mechanism before an accepted lead can be recorded. BookLive entries marked `Posted by ブクログ`, retailer/editorial copy, repost/syndication surfaces, ratings-only or bare reactions, plot/setting summaries, adaptation-only reactions, whole-series or later-volume recollections, and generic single-event comments are rejected. No candidate factor is assigned to a rejected observation. User reviews cannot establish `Art`, a numeric value, `known`, Theme, eligibility, or selection provenance.
