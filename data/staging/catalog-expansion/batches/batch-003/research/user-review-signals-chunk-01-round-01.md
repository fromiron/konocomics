## Screening rule

The review must identify an account or author on the native platform, state an exact early-entry scope (normally volume 1, or an explicitly stated early volume range), and contain a concrete repeated observation that could support a still-unknown text factor. A volume-1 product page by itself is not a stopping point. BookLive entries marked `Posted by ブクログ`, bookstore/editorial copy, ratings-only reactions, adaptation-only reactions, plot/setting summaries, whole-series or multi-volume recollections, anonymous entries, and copied/syndicated text are rejected. A single bounded review is retained as a lead only when its mechanism is relevant; it does not meet the two-independent-review threshold. User reviews cannot establish `Art`, eligibility, selection provenance, or a catalog value.

The candidate-factor column is intentionally `none` whenever the observation is only a plot summary, a known factor, an unbounded impression, or an uncorroborated lead for the residual target.
