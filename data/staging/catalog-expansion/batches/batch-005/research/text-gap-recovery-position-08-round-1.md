The official volume-1 synopsis establishes an initially mysterious transforming
entity, while the volume-2 rightsholder synopsis binds the transformation and
acquisition question to the next entry volume. Two separately authored direct
BookLive records describe bounded first-volume surprise: one points to the
final-panel change in understanding, and the other identifies the unexpected
sphere-to-wolf-to-boy chain and the need for the next-volume synopsis to make

2, “some secrets or reversals,” rather than level 4: the packet does not show a
recurring clue network or a sustained deduction/reveal structure.

describes gradual mystery disclosure. It also does not treat the existence of a
mysterious sphere alone as a reveal. Independent adjudication must decide
whether the two direct review observations describe an actual entry-range
reveal payoff or only reader surprise; if the latter, retain `unknown`.
