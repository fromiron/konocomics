summary. It repeats the bounded cohabitation, marriage wish, former-lover email,
and earthquake pressure already present in the three official volume pages. The
award page adds no recurring Dictionary Theme and does not describe growth,
constraint analysis, a plan, clue solving, or a functional world rule. No new
cell is proposed. The professor and employment details remain background, not
`school` or `workplace` Theme evidence.

### Position 22 — リューシカ・リューシカ

The SQUARE ENIX rightsholder page confirms the ordinary-day premise and describes
the work as an imaginative girl's daily life. This is compatible with the
existing `sliceOfLife` Genre and `pacing=0`; it does not establish recurring

or a concrete comedy/warmth/romance observation. No new cell is proposed.

### Position 23 — 千年万年りんごの子

The Culture Agency's 16th Manga Division critique identifies the work as a
limited-space story and discusses pressure and release in its handling of time

`progression`, `problemSolving`, or `strategy` in the Dictionary sense. The
existing special boundary remains: village ritual cannot be substituted with
`politics` or `historicalReconstruction`. No new cell is proposed.

that 番太郎 and 勇次郎 undergo human growth after contact with 百舌谷. The
classification supports a Genre proposal; the growth wording is too broad to

**Proposal for independent adjudication:**

The publisher's medical-sounding fictional premise is not used for safety or a
mental-health Factor.
