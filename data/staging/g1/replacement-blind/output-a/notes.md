## 2. Repeated Observable Patterns

- **`beyond-the-clouds`**: Steampunk industrial town setting where mechanic Theo rescues Mia, an amnesiac girl with wings. Core structure revolves around quest exploration across skies (`adventure`, `exploration`), mechanical repair/tinkering (`crafting`), and gentle emotional bonding (`foundFamily`). Pacing and worldbuilding are moderate; art factors (`artRealism`, `artDensity`, `visualSoftness`, `motionImpact`) are intentionally excluded per matching specification.
- **`chis-sweet-adventures`**: Domestic slice-of-life animal comedy featuring kitten Chi's interactions with the Yamada family and neighborhood pets. High comedy frequency (`comedy` = 3) and maximum warmth (`emotionalWarmth` = 4), with zero mental stress or strategic narrative elements.
- **`fairy-tail`**: High-energy fantasy guild adventure centered on Lucy joining Natsu and Happy in Fairy Tail. Frequent magic combat (`combat`), guild job assignments (`workplace`), comedic slapstick (`comedy` = 3), and strong team camaraderie (`foundFamily`).
- **`fire-force`**: Steampunk sci-fi action where Shinra joins Special Fire Force Company 8 to combat Infernal combustion outbreaks. Features high-stakes investigation of human combustion (`investigation`), pyrokinetic combat (`combat`), dark tragic themes (`darkness` = 3), and structured company firefighting (`workplace`).
- **`lovely-muco`**: Wholesome pet slice-of-life focusing on dog Muco and glassblower Komatsu at GLASS STUDIO amato. Characterized by high comedy (`comedy` = 3), domestic pet warmth (`emotionalWarmth` = 4), and minimal plot complexity.
- **`noragami-stray-god`**: Supernatural fantasy featuring homeless god Yato, spirit-slipping high schooler Hiyori, and sword Regalia Yukine. Combines sword combat (`combat`), god lore and Shinki corruption (`worldBuilding` = 3), teenage identity growth (`characterArcWeight` = 3), and comedic odd-job antics (`comedy` = 3).
- **`penguin-and-house`**: Quiet comedic domestic slice-of-life where Pen the penguin manages chores, laundry, and pancake cooking (`cooking`) for human roommate Hayakawa. High warmth (`emotionalWarmth` = 4) and gag humor (`comedy` = 3).
- **`sheetas-little-big-world`**: Miniature world adventure following tiny human Sheeta journeying outside his hollow log town. High worldbuilding scale contrast (`worldBuilding` = 3), gentle survival/exploration (`adventure`, `exploration`, `survival`), and maximum healing warmth (`emotionalWarmth` = 4).
- **`the-seven-deadly-sins`**: High fantasy adventure where Princess Elizabeth recruits exiled tavern keeper Meliodas (Dragon Sin of Wrath) and his companions to overthrow corrupt Holy Knights. Features intense battle magic (`combat`), traveling tavern quest (`adventure`), political coup background (`politics`), and cheerful camaraderie.
- **`tokyo-revengers`**: High-stakes time-leap delinquent drama. Takemichi leaps 12 years back to middle school to save Hinata. Heavy mental pressure (`mentalStress` = 4), rapid pacing (`pacing` = 4), character arc weight (`characterArcWeight` = 4), gang brawls (`combat`), timeline analysis (`investigation`), and time travel mechanics (`timeTravel`).

## 3. Unknown Limitations

- Per Rule 2 of the annotation guidelines, absence of explicit data within the inspected entry scope (Volumes 1–3 / first major episode) is never treated as a low value. If a repeated pattern could not be established strictly from the inspected scope, `state` would be recorded as `unknown`.
- In this annotation batch, all 13 non-art axes across all 10 candidates contained observable, sufficient evidence from the official synopses and official first-chapter reader inspection ranges to determine definitive `known` states.
- Art group factors (`artRealism`, `artDensity`, `visualSoftness`, `motionImpact`) were explicitly excluded from scoring matching per Rule 5.
